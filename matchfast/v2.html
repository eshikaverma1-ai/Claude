How MatchFast ranks

Eight published criteria. The short version is here; the full methodology, with every data source, is published.

How are companies ranked?

On eight published criteria: verified review volume and rating, the share of work that matches the ranking, a checked head office, published pricing and minimum project size, client-reported project size, trading history, independent recognition and named client evidence.

Can a company pay to rank higher?

No. Placement is editorial and cannot be bought. Companies can submit themselves for consideration, which does not affect their position.

Where do the figures come from?

Ratings, review counts, hourly bands and minimum project sizes are read from live Clutch profiles and dated on every ranking. Head offices and contracting entities are read from each company's own website.

Why can a 4.8 rank above a 5.0?

Because review volume counts. A 4.8 from 200 clients is stronger evidence than a 5.0 from ten, so ratings are weighted by the number of reviews behind them.

How often are rankings updated?

Each ranking shows the date its figures were read. Review counts are re-checked before a page is updated, and any correction is stated on the page itself.
